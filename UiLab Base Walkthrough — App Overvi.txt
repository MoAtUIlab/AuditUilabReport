UiLab Base Walkthrough — App Overview
What it is
UiLab Base Walkthrough is an internal field-audit and client-reporting web app for UiLab, an Australian applied-AI consultancy. It is a Progressive Web App (PWA) that runs in a browser and can be installed on a phone home screen.

The app lets a small team visit industrial/manufacturing sites, capture what they see, and turn that into a polished, client-ready executive dossier.

Why it exists
Before this app, walkthrough notes were scattered, evidence photos were pasted as links, reports were rebuilt manually, and there was no shared view of which clients had read their reports.

The app solves four problems:

On-site capture — record findings, opportunities, photos, and GPS location while still at the client site, even without internet.
Structured output — turn rough notes into a consistent audit format (maturity scores, findings, opportunities, roadmap).
Client delivery — send either a PDF or a private landing page with a PIN.
Follow-up intelligence — see who opened the report, who printed it, and who needs chasing.
Who uses it
Internal team — anyone with the single shared site password. No individual sign-ups.
Team profiles — named profiles (e.g. Ash, Mo) can be added; each audit is attributed to a profile so you can see who ran which walkthrough.
Clients — receive a private link and 4-digit PIN to view their report. They cannot edit anything.
Core workflow
Arrive on site — open the PWA, start a new walkthrough.
Pin location — capture GPS coordinates and a location label.
Paste notes or capture live — dump rough notes, voice transcripts, or bullet points; AI structures them into a draft audit. Or fill the audit manually.
Add evidence — take or upload photos; they are stored in cloud file storage and shown in the report.
Score and prioritise — rate automation maturity, severity of findings, and effort/impact of opportunities.
Review the dossier — a print-optimised report is generated with brand colours, cover page, running headers/footers, and page-break control.
Deliver to client — choose one of two paths:
PDF download — save to device and email manually (not tracked).
Landing page + 4-digit PIN — client opens in browser, can print/download/share; every open and print is logged.
Follow up — dashboard shows opens, stale links, and reminders to follow up, with Yes/No outcome tracking.
What is inside the app
Pages/routes
/unlock — shared password gate.
/ — dashboard with walkthrough register, team section, engagement metrics, and follow-ups.
/audits/$id — full audit editor with tabs for intake, maturity, findings, opportunities, evidence, roadmap, and deliver.
/audits/$id/report — internal preview of the client dossier, with Print/PDF and share controls.
/profiles/$profileId — profile page showing all audits attributed to that team member.
/r/$token — public client report landing page, PIN-protected, no search indexing.
Key features
Shared password auth — one password unlocks the whole site; no per-user accounts.
Team profiles — add/remove profiles; audits can be assigned to a profile.
AI note-to-audit — paste rough notes; AI drafts client, site, industry, headcount, executive summary, scope, maturity scores, findings, opportunities, and recommendations.
AI executive summary — refresh the summary from the filled-in audit.
Autosave — edits save automatically with a status indicator.
Drag-to-reorder — findings, opportunities, and recommendations can be reordered.
Photo evidence — camera/file upload, signed URLs, gallery view, captions, area tags.
GPS capture — latitude, longitude, location label, timestamp.
Offline support — audits and photos queue on the device when there is no signal; they sync when connection returns.
Print/PDF optimisation — brand colours preserved, no browser URL/footer, page-break control, running header/footer.
Client delivery — two branches: PDF download or PIN-protected landing page.
Engagement metrics — opens, prints, open rate, average time to open, stale links, weekly chart, conversion funnel.
Follow-up reminders — Yes/No confirmation with outcome notes (meeting booked, progressed, no response, not interested, other).
PWA — installable on phone home screen, works offline, branded icon.
Data model
profiles — team members.
audits — the main walkthrough record, including JSON arrays for maturity, findings, opportunities, photos, recommendations, plus GPS fields.
audit_shares — client share links with token, 4-digit PIN, expiry, revocation.
report_views — every open, print, and download event on a client link.
follow_ups — reminders created from report activity, with status and outcome.
Brand/design
Colours: Sunset burgundy #4C0027, Summer orange #FF6325, Sun yellow #FFCD0E, Bone #F5F1EA.
Rule: background is always darker than foreground.
Fonts: Inter Tight / PP Neue Montreal for headings, Roboto Mono for labels.
Style: dark, minimal, Apple-like.
Tech stack
Framework: TanStack Start v1 (React 19, full-stack, SSR/SSG, Vite 7).
Styling: Tailwind CSS v4.
Backend/database: Lovable Cloud (Supabase-managed).
Storage: private evidence-photos bucket with signed URLs.
AI: Lovable AI Gateway, model google/gemini-3.8-flash.
PWA: vite-plugin-pwa, service worker with NetworkFirst navigation and asset caching.
Auth: shared site password via encrypted cookie session.
Outcomes and benefits
Consistency — every walkthrough produces the same structured dossier.
Speed — AI turns rough notes into a first draft in seconds.
Accountability — audits attributed to team profiles; metrics show who is following up.
Client experience — professional, branded report with a private, easy-to-share link.
Sales intelligence — know which clients engaged, which reports went stale, and where deals progressed.
Remote-site reliability — capture data offline; sync when back in signal.
Current state
Phase 1 and Phase 2 are complete.
Email alerts when a client opens a report are blocked until a sending email domain is configured.
Google Analytics for public report pages was planned but the connector request was rejected; internal database metrics remain the source of truth.
Important notes for export
The app expects a SITE_PASSWORD secret and a SESSION_SECRET for the shared-password gate.
The AI feature requires a LOVABLE_API_KEY.
Photo storage uses a private bucket; signed URLs expire after 6 hours.
The PWA service worker only registers in production, not in Lovable preview hosts.
